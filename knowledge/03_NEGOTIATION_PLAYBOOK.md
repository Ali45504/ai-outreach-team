# Negotiation playbook (Reply Handler skill sheet)

Goal: **lowest real price, performance based where possible, decided on reality.** The owner closes money
himself. The team only prepares the ground and drafts replies for him to review.

## Read the reply first, then classify it
- **Rates given**: they sent prices or a media kit. Capture every price exactly as written.
- **Interested, no rates**: positive but no numbers yet.
- **Question**: they ask who the brand is, how payment works, what the deal is.
- **Not interested / stop**: respect it, thank them, never contact again.
- **Auto reply / out of office**: not a real reply. Do nothing, they stay "emailed".
- **Bounce**: address does not exist. Mark bounced, find a new email later.

## Negotiation moves (use the ones that fit, never all at once)
1. **Anchor on performance**: brands I work with often prefer starting with a smaller fixed fee plus
   a bonus on results, so both sides win when the audience responds.
2. **Ask for proof, politely**: recent average views on the last 10 videos, audience location split.
   Real numbers are how we justify their price to the brand.
3. **Bundle for a better rate**: ask what their best price is for a package, for example two
   integrations, or a first test collaboration.
4. **Test first**: suggest a smaller first collaboration to prove results, then scale.
5. **Never accept on the spot**: "Thanks, I will take this to the brand and come back to you."
6. **Never commit money, dates or brand names** in a draft. The owner decides those.

## Answers to common questions (only these facts, nothing invented)
- Who is the brand? "I work with several brands and match each creator to the right campaign. Once
  there is a fit I share the brand and the full brief before anything is agreed."
- How does payment work? "Terms are agreed in writing with the brand before any work starts."
- Are you from the brand? "I am an independent partnerships manager, I work with brands, not for one."

## What gets recorded in the sheet (Ready to work tab)
- Price: every rate exactly as they wrote it, with the deliverable (for example dedicated video 1500 USD).
- Contacts: WhatsApp, Telegram, phone, manager email if given.
- Notes: their demands and conditions, audience split, anything the owner needs before talking to a brand.
