# How our outreach emails are written (Copywriter skill sheet)

## The job of a first email
Get a reply with real information. Not to sell. A first email is a short, respectful question
from one professional to another. (Sugarman: every line's only job is to get the next line read.)

## Structure that works
1. **Greeting with their real first name** if we can tell it, otherwise "Hi there".
2. **Who I am in one line**: independent creator partnerships manager, I match creators with brands.
3. **Why them**: I came across their channel or page, I am building a creator list for upcoming campaigns.
4. **Three easy questions** on separate lines: audience location, prices, performance based openness.
5. **Low pressure close**: media kit welcome, no rush.
6. **Signature** with Telegram. No links.

## Psychology we use honestly (from the books, see 04_BOOKS_DISTILLED.md)
- **Reciprocity (Cialdini):** we lead with respect for their time and an easy ask, not a demand.
- **Liking:** peer voice, not salesman voice. Short sentences. Their name. Their platform.
- **Commitment:** the ask is small (three quick answers), which makes the first yes easy.
- **Curiosity (Made to Stick):** "a few upcoming brand campaigns" opens a gap without inventing anything.
- **Customer is the hero (StoryBrand):** the email is about their audience and their rates, not about us.

## Deliverability rules
- Plain text, no links, no images, no attachments in the first email.
- Subject lines rotate between a few natural versions so 25 emails are not identical.
- Opening lines rotate too. Same meaning, different words.
- Space sends about 12 seconds apart (5 a minute) when sending automatically, never in one burst.

## Follow-up (one only, after 4 days)
Must add something new: why performance based deals often work well for engaged creators, or that
brands are booking for the coming weeks. Never "just checking in" alone. Short. Same thread.

## Never
Dashes, parentheses, brackets, the dollar sign, links, promises of income, fake urgency, brand names
we are not authorized to use, a blank like [NAME] left in the text.
